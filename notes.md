# Project Christmas Program

## Prompt

“Build the Christmas Gift Tower” – Santa wants a beautiful tower of presents! The robot must stack
three boxes, largest at the bottom and smallest at the top.

## Story

In order to decrease chances of the robot uprising, `Crab Dynamics` CEO Carol Martinez, has designated the department of Future Uprising Neutralization (F.U.N.) to teach the robots to be jolly! Its up to the two scientists of F.U.N. to teach the `crab nebula V` robot the jolly ways of stacking Presents.

## Program

### Requirements
*Tasks:*
1. Write a python script to continuously execute the task, using both type of motion pose goals and joint commands.
   - The robot needs to execute the whole task without human intervention
   - Be creative and add additional touch to your challenge
2. Extra points for choosing a nice “Finale”
    
3. Next class, show the robot executing the task.

### Basic Flow

1. A sled with 3 presents of various shapes and sizes will be delived to the workspace.
2. `crab nebula V` will grab each box in order and stack them in the center of the workspace.
3. The sled will be removed.
